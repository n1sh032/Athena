# Dataset

Athena uses the de-identified hand radiographs and bone age annotations from the [2017 RSNA Pediatric Bone Age Challenge](https://www.rsna.org/artificial-intelligence/ai-image-challenge/rsna-pediatric-bone-age-challenge-2017). The challenge page lists a 9.6 GB training download and a 1.1 GB validation download. Bone age is a reference estimate in months. The source's `male` field is recorded metadata used here for aggregate error analysis.

RSNA allows academic, educational, and other non-commercial uses under its [terms of use and attribution](https://www.rsna.org/-/media/files/rsna/education/ai-resources-and-training/ai-image-challenge/rsna-2017-ai-challenge-terms-of-use-and-attribution_final.pdf). Obtain the files from RSNA. Keep raw images, annotations, and per-image predictions out of the repository.

Halabi SS, Prevedello LM, Kalpathy-Cramer J, et al. *The RSNA Pediatric Bone Age Machine Learning Challenge*. Radiology. 2019;290(2):498–503. [Challenge paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6358027/)
